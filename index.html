WHO WE ARE

About Us

Zijani is a renewable energy brand owned and managed by Biogen Diesel Kenya Limited. Zijani aims at reclaiming Used Cooking Oil, Waste and Residues from bulk cooking institutions through a social conscious business voice keen to relay one message: doing the right thing is important, easy and profitable.

TURNING WASTE INTO VALUE

Fry 2 Air

To improve sustainable Bio-feedstock waste collection, Zijani cooperates with the HORECA industry — hotels, restaurants and catering — food processing companies and food delivery platforms. Partners dispose of Used Cooking Oil correctly, contribute to a circular flow of waste disposal and generate an additional revenue stream.
